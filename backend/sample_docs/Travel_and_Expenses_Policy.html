Travel and Expenses Policy

Daily allowance

For domestic travel, the daily allowance is PKR 6,000 per day. For international travel, the daily allowance is USD 80 per day.

Flights

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights over 6 hours with director approval.

Personal car

Using your personal car for work is reimbursed at PKR 45 per kilometre.

Claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the trip, with receipts for every item over PKR 1,000.
